My Dearest Kimi,

When I look back at the years we have built side by side, what strikes me most isn't just the grand celebrations or faraway trips—it is the gentle, quiet beauty of our everyday life. The soft sound of your laughter echoing down the hallway, the comfort of your hand in mine after a long day, and the grace with which you love those around you.

You are my safe harbor in every storm, my loudest cheerleader, and my greatest source of inspiration. You challenge me to become a kinder, stronger, and better man simply by being you. There is no joy in this world that compares to catching your eye across a crowded room and knowing that we belong to each other.

As we celebrate another anniversary today, my promise to you remains as strong as the day we stood before the world: to cherish you, protect your dreams, hold you tight when times get heavy, and love you more passionately with every sunrise we are blessed to share.

Forever and always yours,
Alexander
