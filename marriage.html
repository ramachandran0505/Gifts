Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about wedding deliveries, personalization, and luxury packaging.

Can gifts be delivered directly to the wedding venue or banquet hall?
Yes, absolutely! During checkout, simply provide the wedding venue address, hall name, and recipient contact number. We coordinate with the venue reception to ensure your gift arrives in pristine condition on the scheduled day.
How long does custom name and date engraving take?
All personalized items (LED Plaques, Wooden Frames, Vow Books, Couple Cushions) are precision laser-engraved within 24 to 48 hours. Express shipping ensures doorstep delivery within 2-4 business days across India.
Is luxury gift packaging included with every purchase?
Yes! Every wedding gift includes our signature satin-ribbon gold-foil presentation box and a complimentary handwritten calligraphy card with your personal blessing for the couple.
What if an item is damaged during transit?
We offer a 100% Zero-Risk Transit Guarantee. Every package is sealed in high-density foam cushioning. In the rare event of transit damage, we dispatch an immediate replacement at no cost or issue an instant full refund.
